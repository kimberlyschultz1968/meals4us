Delete your account

You can delete your Meals4Us account and the meal plans saved with it at any time. It takes about a minute.

In the app

  1. Open Meals4Us and look at the row at the top with your email address.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Type your password and confirm.

Or here on the web

Sign in with the email and password you use for Meals4Us, and your account will be deleted.

What is deleted

  • Your account, your email address and your password.
  • Your family profile, meal plans, recipes, grocery lists and everything else saved on our server.
  • Your subscription, if you have one, is cancelled first, so you are not billed again.
  • If you asked us to email you the free 7-dinner plan, that address is removed too.

What is not affected

  • Anything saved on your own phone or computer stays there until you clear it.
  • Payment records held by Stripe are kept by Stripe, as the law requires.

Can't sign in?

Email thebinderapps@gmail.com from the address you used and we will delete your account.
