What's For Dinner This Week?

A real 7-day family dinner plan — quick, kid-friendly, nothing fancy. Free, no sign-up needed.

You already know the feeling. It's 5pm, everyone's hungry, and your brain is completely blank. Here's an easy week to fall back on — and at the bottom, a way to get a new one every week without ever staring at the fridge again.

Your 7-day dinner plan

Sun
🌮 Taco night

Ground beef or chicken, shells, and whatever toppings are in the fridge. Always a win, always fast.

Mon
🍗 Sheet-pan chicken & veggies

One pan, one clean-up. Toss chicken thighs and chopped potatoes/carrots with oil and salt, roast 35 min.

Tue
🍝 Spaghetti & meat sauce

Jarred sauce is fine. Make a double batch of meatballs and freeze half for a night you don't feel like cooking at all.

Wed
🥪 Breakfast for dinner

Eggs, bacon, pancakes. Takes 15 minutes, kids love it, and it breaks up the week.

Thu
🍛 Chicken fried rice

Use up whatever rice and vegetables are left over from earlier in the week. Nothing goes to waste.

Fri
🍕 Pizza night

Delivery, frozen, or homemade on flatbread — Friday is for not cooking much.

Sat
🌭 Free day

Leftovers, eat out, or whatever sounds good. Every week needs one night with no plan at all.

4 tips that actually make meal planning stick

Keep 5 "safety net" meals on repeat

You don't need 30 different dinners. Most families genuinely eat the same 8-10 meals on rotation — figure out yours and lean on them.

Build the grocery list from the plan, not the other way around

Deciding meals first means you buy exactly what you need — less waste, fewer "what do I even make with this" ingredients sitting in the fridge.

Always keep one "free day"

A week with zero flexibility is a week you'll abandon by Wednesday. Leave one night open on purpose.

Write down what actually got eaten

The meals your family devours vs. the ones that got picked at — that's the real signal for next week's plan.
